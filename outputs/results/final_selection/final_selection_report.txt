Limitation — classes F and Q
----------------------------
F and Q are reported in the macro-F1 and in the confusion matrix. Their low scores are a limitation of this final model, not a filtered result.
DS2 F: precision=0.0506, recall=0.3789, F1=0.0893, support=388. Fusion beats are rare and morphologically overlap N and V, so the model does not separate them reliably under the patient-wise protocol.
DS2 Q: precision=0.0000, recall=0.0000, F1=0.0000, support=7. After pacemaker records are removed, Q is extremely scarce and is concentrated in few recordings. A patient-wise split therefore gives the classifier almost no stable Q examples, and a low Q score should be discussed as a data limitation.
